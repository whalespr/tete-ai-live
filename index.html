How the Draw Score works

Draws are the hardest outcome in football — about 26% of games. "High probability" means 28–35%, not 70%. The score rewards agreement across signals.

  • ★Banker — Score ≥ 72 + draw prob ≥ 30%. Highest conviction. Rare: 0–3 a day.
    Best candidates on the board. Still not a guarantee.
  • ●Strong — Score ≥ 62 + draw prob ≥ 28%. High confidence, broad signal agreement.
    Solid draw picks.
  • ◐Lean — Score ≥ 45. Worth a look, thinner edge.
    Consider but be cautious.
  • ○Avoid — Below threshold. Not recommended as a draw.
